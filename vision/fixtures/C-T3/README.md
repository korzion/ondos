# C-T3 Maze scan

Requirement: C-R3

**Method:** 10 scans per test maze under lab light, plus one scan with the ball on the exit marker.

**Pass if:** ≥ 8 of 10 fully correct; invalid or unstable scans not published; ball on the exit: exit_hidden, nothing published.
